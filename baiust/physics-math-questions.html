Physics Practice Questions

Electricity and Magnetism

1. What is the magnitude of the electric field strength \(E\) such that an electron, placed in the field, would experience an electrical force equal to its weight?[cite: 1]
2. Charges of \(6 \times 10^{-9}\text{ C}\), \(-12 \times 10^{-9}\text{ C}\), and \(+14 \times 10^{-9}\text{ C}\) are placed at three corners of a square respectively[cite: 1]. What amount of charge is to be placed at the fourth corner so that the potential at the centre of the square becomes zero?[cite: 1]
3. In each corner of a square of side of \(1\text{ m}\), \(5 \times 10^{-9}\text{ C}\) charge is given[cite: 1]. Calculate the potential at the centre of the square[cite: 1].
4. Two positive electric charges, one 2 times as strong as the other, exert a force of \(8 \times 10^{-5}\text{ N}\) on each other when they are placed \(0.05\text{ m}\) apart in air[cite: 1]. Find the magnitude of each charge[cite: 1].
5. A plastic ball of charge \(3.23 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}\) is kept hanging in a uniform electric field of \(2.6 \times 10^4\text{ V m}^{-1}\)[cite: 1]. If the acceleration due to gravity at that place is \(10\text{ m s}^{-2}\), find the mass of the ball[cite: 1].
6. Two point charges of \(15\text{ C}\) and \(30\text{ C}\) are placed at \(10\text{ m}\) apart from each other[cite: 1]. Find the point on the straight line joining them where the magnitude of electric field intensity will be zero[cite: 1].
7. A plane-parallel plate capacitor has circular plates of radius \(r = 10\text{ cm}\), separated by a distance \(d = 1\text{ mm}\)[cite: 1]. How much charge is stored on each plate when their electric potential difference has the value \(V = 100\text{ V}\)?[cite: 1]
8. A certain parallel-plate capacitor consists of two plates, each with area \(200\text{ cm}^2\), separated by a \(0.4\text{ cm}\) air gap[cite: 1].
(i) Compute its capacitance[cite: 1].
(ii) If the capacitor is connected across a \(500\text{ V}\) source, what is the charge on it, the energy stored in it, and the value of \(E\) between the plates?[cite: 1]
9. A nickel wire of length one metre and diameter \(0.55\text{ cm}\) has resistance \(2.87 \times 10^{-3}\ \Omega\)[cite: 1]. If an e.m.f. of one volt is applied between two ends of the wire, calculate:[cite: 1]
(i) Current through the wire[cite: 1]
(ii) Current density[cite: 1]
10. A metal rod of length \(20.3\text{ cm}\) has resistance \(85 \times 10^{-6}\ \Omega\)[cite: 1]. If the diameter of the rod is \(0.5\text{ cm}\), calculate the resistivity of the metal[cite: 1].
11. The resistance of a wire of length of \(0.48\text{ m}\) and diameter of \(0.12\text{ mm}\) is \(15\ \Omega\)[cite: 1]. Calculate the specific resistance of the material of the wire[cite: 1].
12. If an electron is moving with velocity of \(10^5\text{ m s}^{-1}\) making angle of \(60^\circ\) with a uniform magnetic field of \(0.50\text{ T}\), calculate the force acting on it[cite: 1].
13. A proton is moving with velocity of \(2 \times 10^6\text{ m s}^{-1}\) making angle of \(30^\circ\) with a uniform magnetic field[cite: 1]. If the acting force on proton is \(4.8 \times 10^{-15}\text{ N}\), calculate the magnitude of magnetic field[cite: 1].
14. A circular coil of radius \(40\text{ mm}\) consists of 250 turns of wire in which the current is \(20\text{ mA}\)[cite: 1]. What is the magnetic field in the centre of the coil?[cite: 1]
15. A coil of radius \(200\text{ mm}\) is to produce a field of \(0.4\text{ G}\) in its centre with a current of \(0.25\text{ A}\)[cite: 1]. How many turns must there be in the coil?[cite: 1]
16. Number of turns of a coil is 100[cite: 1]. Magnetic flux of each turn changes from \(30 \times 10^{-5}\text{ Wb}\) to \(2 \times 10^{-5}\text{ Wb}\) when the coil is moved away from a magnet in \(0.04\text{ s}\)[cite: 1]. Calculate the induced electromotive force in the coil[cite: 1].
17. The flux linking an air-cored coil of 500 turns changes from \(30\ \mu\text{Wb}\) to \(60\ \mu\text{Wb}\) in \(2\text{ ms}\)[cite: 1]. Calculate the value of the emf induced in the coil[cite: 1].

Heat and Thermodynamics

1. Calculate the mean free path and the collision frequency for air molecules at \(0\ ^\circ\text{C}\) and \(1\text{ atm}\) pressure. Given the effective diameter of air molecule \(= 2\text{ \AA}\), the root mean square speed of air molecules at N.T.P. is about \(1 \times 10^5\text{ cm/sec}\), and the number of air molecules per c.c. \(= 3 \times 10^{19}\).
2. Calculate the number of molecules per c.c. of a gas if the mean free path of the molecules is \(2.4 \times 10^{-6}\text{ cm}\) and the molecular diameter is equal to \(2.0 \times 10^{-8}\text{ cm}\). What will be the collision frequency if the r.m.s. speed of the molecules under the given conditions is of the order of \(1 \times 10^5\text{ cm/sec}\)?
3. Calculate the root mean square velocity of a molecule of mercury vapour at \(300\text{ K}\). (Given: molecular weight of mercury \(= 221\))
4. Particles of mass \(6.2 \times 10^{-17}\text{ kg}\) are suspended in a liquid at \(27\ ^\circ\text{C}\), and the r.m.s. speed is found to be \(1.4 \times 10^{-2}\text{ m/s}\). Find Avogadro's number from the equipartition theorem. (\(R = 8.314\text{ J/mole-K}\)).
5. Find the efficiency of a Carnot engine working between the steam point and the ice point.
6. Find the efficiency of a Carnot engine working between \(127\ ^\circ\text{C}\) and \(27\ ^\circ\text{C}\).
7. A Carnot engine whose low temperature reservoir is at \(7\ ^\circ\text{C}\) has an efficiency of \(50\%\). It is desired to increase the efficiency to \(70\%\). By how many degrees should the temperature of the high-temperature reservoir be increased?
8. The efficiency of a Carnot engine is \(1/6\). If on reducing the temperature of the sink by \(65\ ^\circ\text{C}\), the efficiency becomes \(1/3\), find the temperatures of the source and the sink between which the engine is working.
9. A Carnot engine whose temperature of the source is \(400\text{ K}\) takes \(200\text{ calories}\) of heat at this temperature and rejects \(150\text{ calories}\) of heat in the sink. What is the temperature of the sink? Calculate the efficiency of the engine.
10. A Carnot engine operates between two reservoirs at temperatures of \(450\text{ K}\) and \(350\text{ K}\). If the engine receives \(1\text{ kcal}\) of heat from the source in each cycle, calculate:
(i) the amount of heat rejected to the sink in each cycle,
(ii) the efficiency of the engine, and
(iii) the work done by the engine in each cycle.
11. Calculate the change in entropy when \(5\text{ kg}\) of water at \(100\ ^\circ\text{C}\) is converted to steam at the same temperature. (Given: latent heat of steam \(= 540\text{ cal/gram}\))
12. Calculate the change in entropy when \(10\text{ grams}\) of ice at \(0\ ^\circ\text{C}\) is converted to water at the same temperature. (Given: latent heat of ice \(= 80\text{ cal/gram}\))
13. Calculate the increase in entropy of \(10\text{ kg}\) of water at \(100\ ^\circ\text{C}\) when it changes to vapour.
14. When \(50\text{ g}\) of water is heated from \(10\ ^\circ\text{C}\) to \(90\ ^\circ\text{C}\), by how much does its entropy change?
